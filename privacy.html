Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how English Reading Room ("we") handles information in its publishing tool, ERR Uploader, and on this website.

What ERR Uploader does

ERR Uploader is an internal tool used only by English Reading Room to upload our own educational videos to our own YouTube channel and to set their titles, descriptions and publishing times. It is not offered to the public and no other people sign in to it.

Google and YouTube data we access

With our own permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and update video details on the English Reading Room channel (scope youtube.upload). It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, other channels or other Google users.

How the data is stored

The only stored item is our own access token, kept as an encrypted secret in our private automation settings. It is never shared, sold or used for advertising, and it is used only to upload our videos.

YouTube

ERR Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using our channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the tool's access at any time in Google security settings.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms and does not collect personal data. If you write to us by email, we use your message only to answer you.

Children

Our videos are educational stories for learners of English. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
